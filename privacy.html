Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026.

PROJECT 99 processes the camera image locally in your browser to estimate facial expression. We do not upload or store your face image or video.

For the daily global ranking, we store an anonymous user identifier generated by our authentication provider, the challenge date, expression type, score, and technical timestamps. This is used to calculate your rank and prevent one anonymous identity from creating multiple scores for the same daily challenge.

Your browser may also store local data such as your best score, streak, and anonymous session so the app can remember your progress.

For basic product analytics, PROJECT 99 may record anonymous events such as a page visit, challenge start, completed challenge, share action, and a short traffic-source label such as TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Google, YouTube, WhatsApp, or direct. These records do not contain your face image or video.

Ranking and authentication infrastructure is provided by Supabase. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages. These providers may process technical information such as IP address and request metadata under their own policies.

PROJECT 99 is prepared to use Google AdSense for advertising. When ads are enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage, device information, IP address and similar technical data to deliver, measure and protect advertising. Where legally required, consent controls will be shown before non-essential advertising storage is used.

You can clear local PROJECT 99 data by clearing this site's browser data. Anonymous ranking records may remain in the service database unless deleted by the operator.

PROJECT 99 is an experimental entertainment challenge and scores are not biometric identification, medical assessment, or a scientific measure of emotion.
