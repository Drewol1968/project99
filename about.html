HOW IT WORKS

One face.
One score.

PROJECT 99 is a quick daily challenge. Copy the target expression, let the browser compare visible facial-expression signals, and see how your score ranks globally.

1. Copy the face

The same daily expression is shown to players worldwide. You can also practice with random faces.

2. Camera analysis stays on your device

The live camera image is processed in your browser. PROJECT 99 does not upload or store your face image or video.

3. Get a score

The app compares expression-related facial signals with the target. The score is an entertainment score, not biometric identification and not a scientific measure of emotion.

4. Climb the ranking

Daily scores are stored with an anonymous account identifier so the app can calculate a global position and show a top leaderboard.

5. Challenge friends

Share the daily challenge or your result. Shared links point back to PROJECT 99 so friends can try the same challenge.

PO POLSKU

Jak działa PROJECT 99?

Codziennie wszyscy dostają tę samą minę. Kamera analizuje wyraz twarzy lokalnie w przeglądarce, bez wysyłania obrazu twarzy. Otrzymujesz wynik i miejsce w globalnym rankingu. To zabawa — wynik nie służy do identyfikacji osoby ani oceny zdrowia lub prawdziwych emocji.
